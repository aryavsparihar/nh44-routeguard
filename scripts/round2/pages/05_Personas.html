Team 01 · RouteGuard · Sangam Round 2 (UI/UX)

User research and personas

Who RouteGuard is for, what they do today, and what each of them needs from the app.

Where this comes from

We built these personas from our Round 1 research: a zone-by-zone dossier of NH-44 from news reports and published studies (deck slide 18), a log of past closures with coordinates (data/incidents.csv), a comparison of six existing tools (deck slide 5), and what we know first-hand. One of us lives in Ramban, and our mentor has been stuck there in a closure.

The four people below are proto-personas: composites based on that research, not on interviews yet. The names are made up. Before the final round, Madhusudan and Aryaveer will talk to growers, drivers and the traffic control unit in Ramban and update this document with what they hear.

Personas

GN

Haji Ghulam Nabi

Apple grower, Shopian · Persona 1
Situation
Delicious and Ambri, about 3 truckloads a season. No CA slot of his own.
Goal
Send the lot when the road will stay open, at a price that pays for the season.
Pain
Step 2 blind spot: decides from WhatsApp forwards. CA storage is short, so fruit waits in the orchard shed.
Device
Mid-range Android, Kashmiri and Urdu first, reads short English labels and numbers.
We give
Dispatch Advisor: one card (SEND / HOLD / RAIL / DIVERT) with the reason and how sure we are.
Evidence
Deck slides 2 to 4, refs [1], [3], [5].
TA

Tariq Ahmad

Commission agent / transport coordinator · Persona 2
Situation
Coordinates 18 trucks in peak season from Sopore, books Budgam parcel-train slots when he can.
Goal
Know where every truck is and give scarce rail / CA slots to the lots that spoil first.
Pain
Phone calls to each driver; slots go first-come; nobody sees road risk and trucks together.
Device
Android phone on the road, laptop at the office.
We give
Shipment Board ranked by priority (spoilage × load value), desktop control room.
Evidence
Deck slides 3, 4 (cause 4: 800 to 1,000 MT a day by rail), refs [4], [8].

Gurpreet Singh

Multi-axle truck driver · Persona 3
Situation
12-wheeler between Qazigund and Ramban, often in a queue for a day or more.
Goal
Warn others about debris fast and know when his stretch reopens.

No signal in the gorge, cold hands or gloves, rumours on WhatsApp.

Older Android, low battery, intermittent 2G.

3-tap Report with an offline queue, 48 px+ buttons, zone push alerts.
Evidence
Deck slides 3, 4, 18 (signal drops), ref [12].
RS

Inspector Rajesh Sharma

J&K Traffic Police, TCU Ramban control room · Persona 4
Situation
Issues go / no-go and one-way timings by vehicle type for the corridor.
Goal
One shared picture of risk and stranded trucks; get official word to drivers fast.
Pain
Can only hold trucks back after a slope fails; advisories travel slowly.
Device
Desktop in the control room, phone in the field.
We give
TCU Console: official override that sets zone confidence to High and pushes to drivers in that zone.
Evidence
Deck slides 3, 5 (existing tools), ref [12].

What the personas changed in the design

  • Ghulam Nabi decides before loading, often in bright sun. So the advisor answers in one large word with an icon, and every decision colour pair is at least 7:1 contrast.
  • Tariq juggles 18 trucks. So the board ranks trucks by priority (spoilage × load value) and has a desktop control-room layout.
  • Gurpreet has gloves, a weak phone and no signal in the gorge. So reporting takes 3 taps, buttons are at least 48 px (hazard buttons 112 px), and reports wait on the phone until there is signal.
  • Inspector Sharma needs official word to beat rumours. So police updates are marked differently from driver reports and set the zone's confidence to High.
