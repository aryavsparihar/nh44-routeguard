Style guide

Colours, contrast, type, spacing, icons and the five components. The same values are the variables and styles in our Figma file.

Principles

  • Readable outdoors. Warm, high-contrast colours from our Round 1 deck. Text is at least 4.5:1; the decision banners are at least 7:1.
  • Never colour alone. Every risk level is also a word, a pattern (stripes for medium, cross-hatch for high) and triangles on badges. Each decision has its own icon shape.
  • Big targets. Everything you can tap is at least 48 × 48 px, on an 8 pt grid.
